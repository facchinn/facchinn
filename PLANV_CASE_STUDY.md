## Overview

**Plan V** is a real-world travel platform focused on discovering and planning experiences in Argentina.

I participated as a **Full Stack Developer**, working across the frontend, backend, database, integrations and production maintenance of the platform.

The source code is intentionally not published because it belongs to a client project. This case study only describes my role, the public product and the technologies and responsibilities I worked with.

## My role

- Frontend and backend development
- Responsive web interfaces for desktop and mobile
- Feature development and continuous improvements
- Reservation and availability flows
- Database work with MySQL
- User-facing and internal functionality
- Third-party service integrations
- Production deployments and maintenance
- Bug fixing and troubleshooting in production

### Integrations & infrastructure

`Mercado Pago` · `Cloudinary` · `Transactional email` · `VPS` · `PM2` · `Deployments` · `Backups`

## Product areas

### Experience discovery

The public platform allows users to explore destinations and travel experiences through a visual, responsive interface designed for both desktop and mobile usage.

### Reservations & availability

I worked on functionality related to reservations, availability and the flows required to connect the user-facing experience with application data and business rules.

### Integrations

The platform includes external services used for payments, media management and email communication. Part of my work involved integrating and maintaining these services within the application.

### Production maintenance

Beyond feature development, I also participated in production support: deployments, troubleshooting, bug fixes, database-related issues, backups and continuous improvements.

## What this project demonstrates

- Work on a **real production product**, not only a learning project
- Full Stack development across UI, backend and database layers
- Working with external APIs and third-party services
- Responsive product development for desktop and mobile
- Debugging and maintaining a live application
- Handling client code and project information responsibly
